About

I study how data quality and classification models shape decisions in organisations and the public sector.

I'm a graduate researcher in information systems and data analytics, finishing a Master of Business Information Systems at the Australian Institute of Higher Education in Sydney. My work looks at how the structure and quality of data, and the models built on it, affect the decisions organisations make.

So far that has meant benchmarking machine learning classifiers, modelling business processes in BPMN 2.0 and analysing data governance in public policy. In one project I compared five classifiers on 11,162 bank marketing records, and Random Forest came out ahead at 72.59% accuracy.

I'm now taking this quantitative work into supply chain sustainability. I'm lead author on a study of which AI capabilities are necessary for supply chain decarbonisation, using PLS-SEM and Necessary Condition Analysis, and co-author on a study of AI-enabled analytics and supply chain ambidexterity in Bangladeshi garment factories.
